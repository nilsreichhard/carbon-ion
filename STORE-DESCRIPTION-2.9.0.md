**Short summary:** A precise weather timeline watchface with temperature, rain, clouds, daylight, calendar and battery on one rolling track.

# Carbon Ion

Carbon Ion turns your Pebble into a compact meteogram. One rolling timeline, adjustable from 12 to 48 hours, shows the last few hours and what's coming:

- **Temperature:** a smooth curve shaded in colour bands from freezing to hot, a dashed feels-like line, and the next-24-hour high and low
- **Rain and clouds:** rain intensity bars under the curve and clouds above it, shown as one layer or split into low, mid and high cloud
- **Weather events:** small icons mark thunder, snow, strong wind gusts and fog at the hour they're expected, each with an adjustable sensitivity (for example Strong storms only, Heavy snow only, or dense fog under 200 m)
- **Daylight:** a day and night track with sunrise and sunset markers, solar noon, midnight and moon phases
- **Now needle:** a marker that shows exactly where you are on the timeline

**Calendar and alarms:** connect up to 3 ICS calendars, each with its own colour row. Events appear as a start mark or a full-length block, and recurring events, time zones and DST are handled on your phone. A row can also show your next watch alarm.

**Battery forecast:** Carbon Ion learns how fast your battery drains and marks on the timeline when it will reach 20, 10, 4 and 0 %.

**Weather your way:** free Open-Meteo with a choice of models (MeteoSwiss, DWD, GeoSphere, KNMI, DMI, Météo-France or ECMWF), plus MET Norway and GeoSphere Austria, all without an API key. Tomorrow.io, OpenWeatherMap, WeatherAPI and Meteoblue work with your own free key. The settings page adapts to each provider's limits.

**Made for every screen:** Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2. On the round display the timeline, clouds and temperature bend along the edge as arcs, with icons that follow the curve.

**Also included:** a large clock with date, city, steps, Bluetooth and Quiet Time indicators, light and dark themes, wrist flick to change the range or cloud view, high-contrast colours while the backlight is on, and an honest offline mode that shows when the weather is out of date.

Based on the open-source Carbon watchface by Cory Hughart.
