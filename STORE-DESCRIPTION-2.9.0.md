# Carbon Ion

Carbon Ion turns your Pebble into a compact meteogram. A rolling timeline (12 to 48 hours) shows the past and the coming hours at a glance:

- **Temperature curve** with colour bands, a dashed feels-like line, and the next-24-hour high and low
- **Rain bars and clouds** shown as one layer or split into low, mid and high
- **Weather events** for thunder, strong wind, snow and fog
- **Daylight track** with sunrise, sunset and moon phases
- **Up to 3 calendars** (ICS) plus your next alarm, each in its own colour
- **Battery forecast** with markers at 20 / 10 / 4 / 0 %
- Large clock, date, city, steps, Bluetooth and Quiet Time indicators

**Weather your way:** free Open-Meteo (with MeteoSwiss, DWD, GeoSphere, KNMI, DMI, Météo-France and ECMWF models), MET Norway or GeoSphere Austria, all without a key. Tomorrow.io, OpenWeatherMap, WeatherAPI and Meteoblue work with your own API key. Settings adapt to each provider's limits.

**Made for every screen:** Pebble Time 2, Pebble Round 2 (the timeline curves along the round display) and Pebble 2 Duo.

**Extras:** light and dark themes, wrist flick to change the range or cloud view, high-contrast backlight colours, and an honest offline mode.

Based on the open-source Carbon watchface by Cory Hughart.
