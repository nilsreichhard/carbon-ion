## Carbon Ion 3.2.1

Changes since 3.2.0 (current store version).

**Display**
- Temperature chart labels (Pebble Time 2): the high, current and low temperatures now use the full height of the chart instead of being bunched together. The high sits just below the top, the low just above the hour ticks, and the current temperature is centred with equal gaps above and below. Spacing is based on the visible digits, so it looks even with any combination of numbers. Round watches are unchanged.
